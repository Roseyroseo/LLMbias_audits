The Team

Bias Audit Action Team exists to stress-test the black box. We blend the rigor of data science with the nuance of social science to audit AI systems where they are most vulnerable: their application in education, law, employment, and health. Our mission is to turn statistical red flags into actionable, real-world accountability.
